# Used 0.9.1 acceptance holdouts

This record preserves the two repositories used in an independent post-publication acceptance check of
`orchescope@0.9.1`. They were selected before the 0.9.2 coverage correction and then used to design that
correction. They are regression inputs, not unseen holdouts, at any later revision.

The 0.9.1 passing pair from the [1642f0f6 evaluation](1642f0f6-passed-blind-evaluation.md) is also used
and remains ineligible.

## What a later evaluator must not reuse

Do not select either repository, either owner lineage, or a fork of either tree as an unseen 0.9.2
holdout. Do not select the 0.9.1 passing pair. A new blind evaluation still needs a different unseen
positive and a different unseen negative after the candidate is frozen.
